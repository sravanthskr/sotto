# Sotto — what it does today

*Consider it done.*

Sotto lives on this Windows PC. You talk to it like you'd talk to a person, and it does the work inside your own email, calendar, files and apps. Then it tells you what actually happened — including when something didn't work. It asks before anything that matters, and it never pretends.

**Everything on this page works today, on this machine.** Nothing here is a promise of the future — those are listed honestly at the bottom.

---

## Things you can say today

### Email — your real inbox
- *"What's in my inbox?"* / *"Anything urgent in my email?"*
- *"Read me the one from Sara."*
- *"Reply to Sara that I'll confirm tonight."* → it writes the reply, reads it to you, and sends only when you say so.
- *"Send it."* → it goes out from **your own Google account**, and Sotto confirms it really left.

Yes — this is your actual email, not a practice box. You sign in to Google once, yourself, and you can disconnect any time.

### Your day — calendar
- *"What's tomorrow?"* / *"What's left this week?"*
- *"Put a meeting with Rahul on Thursday at 3."*
- It reads your real calendar and adds events to it.

*(Moving or cancelling an existing meeting is coming next — today it reads and creates.)*

### Files & documents
- *"Find my resume."* — by name, or even if you only remember what's *inside* the file.
- *"What does this PDF say?"* — it reads long documents and tells you the short version.
- *"Clean up my downloads."* — it sorts the mess into folders (and can undo it).
- *"Move / copy / rename this."* — tidying, properly, including the recycle bin instead of permanent deletes.

### What's on your screen
- *"What's this error?"* / *"Read this and tell me what it means."*
- It looks at your screen or an open window **only when you ask** — never on its own.

### Your apps & everyday controls
- *"Open Photoshop."* / *"What's installed on this PC?"* / *"Close that window."*
- Volume, brightness, music that's playing, screenshots, clipboard — the small stuff, spoken.
- Lock, sleep, restart, shut down — with a clear question before it does.

### Notes, reminders, and memory
- *"Note down: milk and eggs."* / *"What are my notes?"*
- *"Remind me at 4 to call the bank."* — it remembers so you don't have to.
- *"Remember that I ship on Fridays."* / *"Forget that."*
- Its memory of you is private, lives on your PC, and you can read and edit it.

### Your routines
- Save a set of steps once — *"Save this as my Monday routine"* — then just say *"Do my Monday routine."*

### Your voice
- It answers out loud. You choose the voice, including a **custom voice** trained on samples you own.
- *"Repeat that."* / *"Say it slower."* / *"Stop."* — it obeys instantly, mid-sentence.
- It speaks sentence by sentence, so it starts replying quickly even when the full answer is long.

### The rundown & the world
- *"Give me my briefing."* — a short spoken overview to start your day.
- *"What's the weather?"* / *"Look this up for me."* — it reads and gives you the short version.

### When a job needs the hard stuff
- Running commands, typing into fields, technical chores — it can handle these too when a job calls for them.

---

## How it behaves (the part that matters most)

- **It asks first** when something has consequences — sending, deleting, shutting down. One clear question, never a wall of questions.
- **It tells you the truth.** If it finished, it says so. If it couldn't, it says that too — plainly. It would rather say "I couldn't confirm it" than guess.
- **It stops the moment you say stop** — and it tells you the state of things after ("stopped; the email is still a draft").
- **It remembers so you don't repeat yourself.**
- **It keeps a record.** Ask *"What did you do today?"* and it can show you what it did, ready to check.

---

## What you need

- A Windows PC (this one).
- Sign in to your own **Google account** once — that's the permission for email and calendar. Nothing leaves your machine without your say-so.
- Bring your own **AI key** — the smarts run on a service of your choice, and it's the only thing you pay for.
- Your passwords and secrets are locked on your PC, not on someone's server.

---

## What it does *not* do yet (the honest list)

- Move or cancel existing meetings — *next.*
- Find your free hours in a week — *next.*
- Long chained errands like *"get me ready for tomorrow's interview"* start-to-finish — *that's the big next piece.*
- WhatsApp and chat apps — *not yet.*
- Macs, phones, other devices — *Windows only for now.*
- Free AI keys can be moody — when the smarts are being slow, Sotto tells you instead of going quiet.
